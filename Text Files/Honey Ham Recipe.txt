For the ham:
2kg smoked boneless gammon joint
500ml Fever-Tree Ginger Ale
100 ml Old J Spiced Rum
1 tsp cloves
1 tsp of black peppercorns
1 orange, sliced

For the glaze:
100 g light brown soft sugar
1 tsp of ground ginger
1 tbsp Dijon mustard
2 tbsp honey
50 ml fresh orange juice (about 1 orange)
40 ml Old J Spiced Rum

Lay orange slices in the base of the slow cooker. Add the gammon, pour over ginger ale + spiced rum, then add cloves & black peppercorns. Cook on low for 4–6 hours, until tender.
Mix all the glaze ingredients together in a bowl until smooth and set aside.
Preheat oven to 190°C / 170°C fan. Remove the ham, peel off the rind and score the fat.
Brush over the glaze and roast for 20–30 minutes until sticky, glossy and golden.
